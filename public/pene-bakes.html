Our Story

A taste of the Caribbean, from our family to yours

Pene Bakes started in a home kitchen with one goal: to share the flavours of Guyana with everyone who missed the taste of home. Every pine tart, every slice of black cake, and every warm cheese roll is made by hand in small batches.

We use traditional recipes and real ingredients — no shortcuts — so each bite brings back the warmth of a Sunday afternoon back home.
